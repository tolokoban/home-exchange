Most of the garbage goes into the trash bins on the cemetary parking (point **1** on the map below).

It's not mandatory (though much appreciated) but you can do selective sorting of waste if you like to.
For this you can use the two black boxes like this:

1. All the glass (bottles, broken glasses, etc.).
2. All the paper, aluminium cans, and plastics.

![Black boxes](image/black-boxes.jpg)

If the boxes are full, you can empty them in the trash bins at the corner of the cemetery.

----

You can also put your vegetable peelings in the compost, for example.

1. A small container is on the windowsill in front of the countertop.
2. When it is full, you can empty it into the black box on the left when you go out through the French door.
3. And if that box is full, you can empty it into the garden compost in the corner of the yard, above the garage.
